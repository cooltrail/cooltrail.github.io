Sky Switcher is a Mac app for changing the Roblox sky. Pick a sky you already have, or drop your own pictures on Up, Down, Left, Right, Front, and Back.

### Install

1. Download the zip below.
2. Double-click the zip. A folder named Sky Switcher opens.
3. Drag **Sky Switcher** into your Applications folder.
4. The first time you open it, right-click the app and choose **Open**, then click **Open** again.

Quit Roblox before you switch skies. If you use AppleBlox, open the game from AppleBlox afterward so the sky stays after a Roblox update.
