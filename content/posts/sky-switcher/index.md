Sky Switcher changes the Roblox sky. Pick a sky you already have, or drop your own pictures on Up, Down, Left, Right, Front, and Back.

Quit Roblox before you switch skies. On a Mac, if you use AppleBlox, open the game from AppleBlox afterward so a Roblox update does not wipe the sky. On Windows, open the game from Bloxstrap or Fishstrap if you use one of those.

### Mac

1. Download the Mac zip.
2. Double-click the zip. A folder named Sky Switcher opens.
3. Drag **Sky Switcher** into your Applications folder.
4. The first time you open it, right-click the app and choose **Open**, then click **Open** again.

### Windows

1. Download the Windows zip.
2. Right-click the zip, open **Properties**, check **Unblock**, and click **OK**. Then unzip it.
3. Double-click **Sky Switcher.bat**.
4. If Windows says it protected your PC, click **More info**, then **Run anyway**.
