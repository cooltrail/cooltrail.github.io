Sky Switcher for Windows

1. Right-click SkySwitcher-Windows.zip, choose Properties, check Unblock, and click OK. Do this before you unzip.
2. Double-click the zip. Open the folder named Sky Switcher Windows.
3. Double-click Sky Switcher.bat.
4. If Windows says it protected your PC, click More info, then Run anyway.
5. Quit Roblox if it is open.
6. Pick a sky and click Use this sky. Or click New sky and drop pictures on the empty panels.
7. Empty sides can stay empty. When every panel has a picture, click Use this sky.

If you use Bloxstrap or Fishstrap, open the game from there after you switch skies.
