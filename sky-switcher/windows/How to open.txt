Sky Switcher for Windows

1. Right-click the zip, open Properties, check Unblock, and click OK.
2. Unzip it.
3. Double-click Sky Switcher.bat.
4. If Windows says it protected your PC, click More info, then Run anyway.
5. Quit Roblox, then pick a sky or make your own.

If you use Bloxstrap or Fishstrap, open the game from there after you switch skies.
